🤖 SmartHire – AI Interview & Job Matching Platform

SmartHire is a web-based AI-powered interview and job matching platform designed to help candidates practice interviews, evaluate their performance, and discover suitable job opportunities.

The platform provides an interactive interview experience with random questions, difficulty-based questions, score tracking, interview history, and job search functionality.
